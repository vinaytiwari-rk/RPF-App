# SAMAHIT Question Bank — Content and Reference Policy

## Reference books

Lucent's General Knowledge and Arihant exam-preparation books may be used as a syllabus/topic checklist or for a human editor's factual cross-check where legally obtained. Do not scan, copy, bulk-transcribe, closely paraphrase, or recreate their proprietary question sets, explanations, tables, or distinctive selection/sequence. The SAMAHIT bank must contain independently authored questions in original wording, with original distractors and explanations.

## Original subject coverage

Build independent question packs for:
- General Knowledge and General Awareness
- English grammar and vocabulary
- Mathematics / Quantitative Aptitude
- General Science
- Social Science and Economics
- Indian Constitution and Polity
- Ancient, Medieval and Modern Indian History
- Indian and World Geography
- Computer Awareness and Digital Safety
- Reasoning and exam-specific practice

Use publicly verifiable facts, official syllabi, public-domain sources, and primary government/institutional references when available. A textbook can suggest a topic to cover, but it is not permission to reproduce the book's questions.

## Current Affairs sources

- PIB: primary source for government announcements; retain the official URL and the date facts were verified.
- Drishti IAS and SarkariTel: discovery/secondary cross-check only unless a permitted feed/API is confirmed.
- The Hindu and Times of India: use links and independently verified facts only until applicable reuse terms or a written licence allow more.
- Do not copy article text, summaries, paywalled material, or publisher question sets into SAMAHIT.
- Each current-affairs item must have source metadata and a review date. Retire or review time-sensitive facts periodically.

## Publication requirements

Every published question must have a stable unique ID, Hindi and English versions, four aligned options per language, a valid correct index, an explanation in both languages, a subject tag, and a review status. Count only actual, validated questions in the published manifest. Do not inflate counts with duplicates, template permutations, or unreviewed generated items.

## Current implementation note

This branch adds 30 independently written bilingual practice questions across Mathematics, English, Indian History, Social Science/Economics-Civics, and Computer Literacy. They are a starter batch, not the 50,000+ target and not yet an independently source-reviewed final exam bank.
